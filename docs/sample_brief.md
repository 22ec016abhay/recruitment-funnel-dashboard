**Headline**  
The overall hire rate for the period is 7.87%, with 118 hires from 1,500 applicants.

**Sources**  
LinkedIn produced the highest number of hires, 27, and a hire rate of 7.03%.  
Referral candidates had the highest hire rate, 19.52%, with 41 hires.  
The Company Website and Indeed sources had lower hire rates, 4.74% and 6.14% respectively, and fewer hires.

**Funnel**  
Candidates drop out most between the Screened and Interviewed stages, where the conversion falls from 56.8% to 53.52%.  
The drop from Interviewed to Offered is also significant, with a conversion of 33.33%.

**Caveats**  
Three cohorts after the mature cutoff remain open, with 358 applicants; their outcomes should not be used to calculate hire rates.  
The Job Fair source has only a few hires, so its hire rate of 2.65% is unstable.
